## Synthesis

The roles converge on four robust points:

1. **Archive-level facts are publishable as facts when row-linked.** The selected checkpoint has 194 scored rows and raw 131/63 verdicts; the archive names 24 separate omissions. The official report marks one provider outage, while two final results have explicit provider-transient terminal notes. Therefore publish the raw result, the report-listed-only sensitivity, and the all-terminal-provider exclusion separately.
2. **Judge modes must stay stratified.** The 72 behavioral-reference rows and 122 compile-only rows are not one validated evaluation population. The campaign's own report calls compile-only exploratory. “131/192” is only a descriptive mixed-mode sensitivity, never a single accuracy claim.
3. **The strongest task-specific success is exact Bayesian inference on a stipulated policy model.** The `epistemic_games` source disclaims fully recursive level-k reasoning; all seven selected answers pass. This is not evidence of recursive strategic reasoning or a new ToM capability.
4. **Nominal difficulty and category labels overpromise.** The source comparator contains unreferenced category-axis placeholders and concrete prompt/judge mismatches. The weird-machine response pattern is observable but one-run, class-name/hint-confounded, and task-specific. The failure taxonomy often records patch, source-validation, action, or runtime errors rather than a clean measure of conceptual understanding.

The roles do not fully agree on whether the archive supports a “capability map.” Theorist and Experimentalist accept a bounded local response profile; Skeptic would foreground evaluation/provenance defects; Prior-Work Killer calls the novelty unestablished. The synthesis resolves this by keeping the map as a descriptive visualization of outputs and error states, not a latent capability ordering.

## Unresolved matters retained for the posts

- The primary performance denominator excludes both final rows explicitly marked provider-transient. The archive's campaign-level outage counter tracks only one. POST-04 must show both, not silently settle the term “outage.”
- The axis placeholder scan is from the clean source comparator and the campaign recorded a dirty repository. It establishes what the selected config values refer to in that snapshot, not exact run-time file identity. Posts must phrase no-op-axis claims as source-comparator findings.
- The per-instance `behavioral_reference` label is not identical to an independently validated oracle. The one environment-level `public_bayes_oracle` self-test applies only to `epistemic_games`.
- The source literature set is targeted rather than exhaustive. No post may state a global “first” or comprehensive novelty claim.
