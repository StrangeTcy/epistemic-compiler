# Council synthesis — Campaign 01

**Process note:** this synthesis follows the four saved role analyses and [`cross_critique.md`](cross_critique.md). All role passes were sequential work by the same Arena Agent, not independent external-model replications; see [`provenance.md`](provenance.md).

## Joint conclusion

The archive supports a row-linked descriptive account of one Atria-Dawn-Preview campaign. It does **not** support a uniform capability score or a general causal claim about difficulty, recursive theory of mind, category-theoretic competence, or weird-machine reasoning. Each publishable result should stay attached to its judge mode, task implementation, selected-case denominator, terminal event, and archive artifact.

## Claims the roles converge on

1. **Keep the denominator sensitivity visible.** The raw checkpoint is 131 PASS/63 FAIL in 194 scored cases; the archive separately names 24 omissions. The report lists one provider outage, but two final results carry explicit provider-transient terminal notes. Thus 131/193 excludes only the report-listed case, while 131/192 excludes both final provider-coded results. This is an analysis policy—not a rewrite of the raw checkpoint. (`F-02`, `F-03`.)
2. **Do not combine unlike judge guarantees without a warning.** The primary 192-case set contains 72 behavioral-reference rows and 120 compile-only rows; the campaign report says compile-only verdicts are exploratory. Per-instance calibration is not interchangeable with an environment-level independent oracle self-test. (`F-04`.)
3. **Describe `epistemic_games` as bounded Bayesian inference.** All seven selected cases pass, but the source directly states that v0 supplies likelihoods for specified policies and does not implement fully recursive level-k belief reasoning. That is a positive result about the specified task, not recursive strategic reasoning. (`F-07`.)
4. **Treat category labels as code-task groupings, not mathematical proof.** The family has 57/84 eligible passes and mixed judge modes; source-comparator examples show differences between prompts, visible tests, hidden checks, and nominal axis placeholders. The repository was dirty, so these are comparator findings, not proven descriptions of every executed workspace. (`F-01`, `F-08`, `F-14`, `F-15`.)
5. **Keep weird-machine results local and confounded.** The six selected environments produce 25/30 eligible passes. The regex length/surface pattern is an observed five-cell result; class-name and hint changes, one seed, and source identity prevent causal attribution. (`F-10`, `F-18`.)
6. **Decompose failures before interpreting scores.** Empty patches, source-validator outcomes, runtime errors, malformed actions, missing companion files, and provider transients are different terminal events. The two `overfit_visible_tests` labels do not match the corresponding missing-file notes and trusted scores. (`F-03`, `F-09`.)

## Remaining disagreement and its resolution

The Theorist and Experimentalist accept a bounded “capability map” if it means observed task outcomes. The Skeptic warns that defects and mixed judges should dominate the headline. The Prior-Work Killer finds no broad methodological novelty and requires much narrower claims. The synthesis resolves this by using “map” only for a descriptive visualization of case-level outputs and failure layers—never a latent ability ranking, validated aggregate, or causal difficulty curve. (`F-16`, `F-17`.)

One speculative hypothesis remains admissible only as a follow-up: on this regex task, altered surface names or the hard-level hint may have mattered more than tested string length. The current data cannot distinguish that possibility from sampling variation, interactions, or unobserved source differences. It is tagged `SPECULATIVE`, not an observed result. (`F-18`.)
