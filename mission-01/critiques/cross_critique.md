## 2. Unresolved Disagreements Register (`UD01`–`UD05`)

Per the **No Averaging Rule** (`research_protocol/protocol.md` §2.3), the following disagreements between Council members are preserved explicitly for Human Gate 1 & Gate 2 arbitration:

### `UD01` — Headline Framing & Title: Bidirectional Oracle Audit (`N03`) vs. Sheaf Gluing Duality (`N01` + `N02` + `N03`)
- **`P01-T` (Theorist) Position**: Retains the unified sheaf/presheaf duality (`Proposition 1`) as the overarching mathematical structure, provided the 3 mathematical corrections are enforced (separating Type 0 vs. Type I-A/B/C vs. Type II; distinguishing `Pairwise-Tree` vs. `Pairwise-Complete` vs. `Pairwise-UpToGauge`; and introducing gauge group $\mathcal{G}$).
- **`P03-S` (Skeptic) & `P04-PW` (Prior-Work Killer) Position**: Because `L06`–`L07` own the mathematics and `L08` (`arXiv:2504.17700`) already proposed sheaf cohomology for multi-agent AI, the **Strongest Unoccupied Novelty Wedge is `N03` (Bidirectional Type I Shortcut Certification + Type II Valid-Solution Rejection Oracle Audit)**, supported by `N01` (placing the presheaf on the *judge* rather than the agent) and `N02` (measuring empirical gauge diversity $\hat{g}/\hat{\kappa}_e$ and marginal yield over `Circular-AG`). Both `P03-S` and `P04-PW` demand that "sheaf/cohomology" be excluded from the paper title unless `ABORT-01` is cleared and `Full-Cocycle` beats `Circular-AG` (`FALS-03b`).
- **Proposed Gate 1 Synthesis for Human Decision**: Re-center Mission 01's primary empirical thesis on **`N03` + `N01` + `N02`**: *"Bidirectional Oracle Auditing and Compositional Gluing in Agent Benchmark Judges: Measuring Type I Shortcut Certification, Type II Valid-Solution Rejection, and Multi-Chart Holonomy"*, carrying the presheaf/gauge formalism (`P01-T-1`..`P01-T-5`) as attributed mathematical notation (`C1`–`C4`) rather than a claimed mathematical invention.

### `UD02` — Part B Execution Scope in Mission 01 V0: Live Multi-Model LLM Sampling (`N >= 30` × 3 Model Families) vs. Two-Track Audit (Deterministic Gauge-Orbit Matrix + Sampled Agent Completions)
- **`P03-S` (`CR02`/`CF04`) & `P04-PW` (`N02`) Position**: Demand sampling $N \ge 30$ completions per chart across $\ge 3$ LLM model families (~1,800–2,880 LLM calls) to measure ecological gauge diversity $\hat{g}$ / $\hat{\kappa}_e$, with `ABORT-04` / negative-result reporting (`C8`) if $\hat{g} < 1.5$.

  - **Track B-Mech (Deterministic Mechanism & Protocol Audit)**: Exact verification of `Chart-Only` vs. `Pairwise-Tree` vs. `Pairwise-UpToGauge` vs. `Circular-AG` vs. `Sheaf-Cocycle` across the real multi-chart environments (`rope`, `rd_state_carry`, `rd_weight_tie`, `rd_adaptive_halting`, `compositional_optimizer`, `sheaf_physical_constraints`) over the enumerated gauge orbits $\mathcal{G}(U_{ij})$, measuring exact holonomy $\operatorname{hol}(\gamma) \in \mathbb{Z}$ and constraint parsimony (`CTRL07`).

## 3. Consolidated Hypothesis Set (`H0`–`H5`) Post-Critique

Incorporating `P01-T`, `P03-S`, and `P04-PW`:

- **`H0` (Null — Local & Pointwise Sufficiency)**: Existing `judge.py` evaluators and the 4-variant oracle gate in `rl_eval_generator` exhibit both low Type I gluing blindness ($\beta_A^{\text{glue}} < 0.20$ on Stratum A1 `REFERENCES`) and low Type II valid-solution rejection ($\gamma_A < 0.10$), and multi-chart `Chart-Only` composition matches `Sheaf-Cocycle` within $10\%$.
- **`H1` (Type I Oracle Gluing Blindness — `N01`/`N03`)**: Under black-box spec-only construction (`CTRL01`), local-projection `judge.py` evaluators falsely certify globally non-gluable **Type I** sections (`Type I-A` horizon truncation, `Type I-B` unanchored gauge, `Type I-C` hollow-cycle holonomy—excluding `Type 0` lookup tables) as `PASS (score = 1.0)` in $\beta_A^{\text{glue}} \ge 50\%$ of audited environments (reported separately on Stratum A1 `REFERENCES` and Stratum A2 `compile_only`), whereas pre-registered `Grader B` restriction-map checks reduce $\beta_B^{\text{glue}}$ to $\le 5\%$.
- **`H2` (Type II Oracle Presheaf Inconsistency / Valid-Solution Rejection — `N03` Primary Wedge)**: Point-probed `judge.py` evaluators over-anchor spec-unanchored gauges ($G_{\text{judge}} \subsetneq G_{\text{spec}}$), falsely rejecting independently verified `reference_alt` valid global sections ($\gamma_A > 0.20$ across the cohort and $\ge 60\%$ rejection across $\ge 3$ independent `reference_alt` variants per environment on Affected environments), whereas `Grader B` achieves $\gamma_B \le 0.05$ (`ABORT-02`).
- **`H3` (Multi-Chart Cycle Holonomy vs. Spanning-Tree & Circular-AG Contracts — `N02`)**: On multi-chart tasks with non-trivial gauge group $\mathcal{G}$ and closed 3-cycles (`rope`, `rd_state_carry`, `sheaf_physical_constraints`, `compositional_optimizer`), `Chart-Only` and `Pairwise-Tree` / `Pairwise-UpToGauge` admit non-zero integer holonomy ($\operatorname{hol}(\gamma) \neq 0$, failure $\ge 20\%$), and explicit cocycle/holonomy enforcement (`Sheaf-Cocycle`) reduces global gluing failure to $< 5\%$ while beating or matching `Circular-AG` (`L11`) at $\le$ constraint predicate count (`CTRL07`).
- **`H4` (Ecological Shared-Prior Gauge Collapse — `P01-T-7` / `N02` Negative-Result Alternative)**: Independent LLM completions on isolated charts concentrate on a single canonical convention per overlap ($\hat{g} < 1.5$, $\hat{\kappa}_e > 0.75$), so ecological multi-agent gluing failure is significantly lower than the uniform combinatorial baseline $1 - \prod_e (1/g_e)$. (Per `C8`, confirmation of `H4` is a valid, publishable negative finding on ecological cycle obstructions.)
- **`H5` (Evaluator–Orchestrator Duality Co-Location — `P01-T-7` / `N01`)**: Across multi-chart tasks, the set of overlap edges $E_J$ where `judge.py` fails to enforce restriction compatibility co-locates with the set of overlap edges $E_O$ where `Chart-Only` sections fail to glue ($\text{Jaccard}(E_J, E_O) \ge 0.70$).
