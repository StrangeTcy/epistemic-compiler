**`L04` — Property-Based Testing (Claessen & Hughes, QuickCheck, ICFP 2000) + Metamorphic Testing (Chen et al., 1998; Segura et al., 2016)**

- **What It Establishes:** Correctness is specified as *universally quantified algebraic laws* and *relations between multiple executions*, rather than fixed input/output pairs. Metamorphic testing specifically exists to handle the **test oracle problem** — verifying programs when no pointwise oracle is available — by asserting consistency relations across related runs.
- **Topical Similarity vs. Actual Overlap:** **Topical similarity: CRITICAL — this is the single most dangerous item for `S01`.** A metamorphic relation across two executions *is* a consistency constraint between two "charts" of the input space. `S01`'s "check $\rho_{ij}(s_i) = \rho_{ji}(s_j)$ instead of probing $t=1$" is, stripped of vocabulary, "replace pointwise oracles with metamorphic relations." **Actual overlap: HIGH on method, LOW on object.** Metamorphic testing relates *executions of one program*; `S01` relates *independently authored modules* and asks about cycle closure. But the burden is now on `S01` to show the cyclic case is not just a metamorphic relation with three arguments.
- **Naive Claim It Kills:** "Replacing fixed-point judge probes with relational/law-based consistency checks is novel." Dead since 1998/2000.

**`L07` — Sheaf-Theoretic Contextuality (Abramsky & Brandenburger, 2011; Abramsky, Barbosa, Kishida, Lal, Mansfield, 2015) + Acyclic Join Dependencies (Fagin, Mendelzon & Ullman, 1982; Beeri, Fagin, Maier & Yannakakis, 1983)**

### **`N01` — The Evaluator-as-Presheaf Duality: the judge, not the agent, as the non-gluing object**

**Exact defensible claim:**
> *Prior applications of sheaf-theoretic local-to-global reasoning in AI (`L08`, `L09`) treat **the system under study** — agents, policies, graph signals — as the object carrying the presheaf. We instead place the presheaf structure on **the evaluator**: a benchmark judge is modeled as a family of local assertions over a cover of the task specification, and judge pathology is read off as (i) failure to probe overlaps ($\rho_{ij}$ never evaluated) and (ii) internal inconsistency between its own local assertions. The contribution is the observation that **evaluator false-positive certification (`Dual A`) and multi-agent integration failure (`Dual B`) are the same cochain-level defect viewed from opposite sides**, instantiated and measured in one harness.*

**What was surrendered vs. pass 1:** The "5-variant oracle gate" is **no longer claimed as apparatus novelty** (`K05`). It is demoted to an *implementation detail* of the audit. The "sheaf duality for multi-agent AI" framing is **no longer claimed at all** (`L08`).

**Why a frontier-safety researcher should care:** Every control/eval protocol (`L12b`) rests on a trusted verifier. This reframes "is my verifier adequate?" from an unbounded test-coverage question into a **structural** one: *does the verifier ever evaluate the overlaps, or only the interiors?* That is a checkable property of a judge's source, auditable before any model is run — complementary to `L01`/`L02`, which can only detect inadequacy *after* a model exploits it.

**Survival condition:** collapses to zero if the draft cannot articulate a judge-side diagnostic that EvalPlus-style input augmentation (`L01`) cannot reach.

### **`N02` — Measured marginal yield of cocycle enforcement over contract-based baselines, with *ecologically observed* gauge diversity**

**Exact defensible claim:**
> *We provide the first **quantitative** measurement, on a fixed multi-chart environment registry with LLM-agent-generated local sections, of the **marginal** global-gluing-failure reduction attributable to each successive protocol strength: `Chart-Only` → `Pairwise-Spanning` → `Circular-AG` (`L11` baseline) → `Full-Cocycle`. We additionally report the **empirically observed gauge diversity** $\hat{g}$ — the number of distinct mutually-incompatible local conventions that independent frontier agents produce per chart — rather than injecting gauge choices by construction.*

**Why this survives `L07`, `L08`, `L11`:** Database theory (`L07`) proved cyclic pairwise-consistency failure is *possible*; `L08` proposed studying it in multi-agent AI; `L11` provided a non-sheaf method for cyclic composition. **None of them measured whether it happens, how often, or whether cocycle checking adds anything over circular A-G, when the local sections are produced by LLMs rather than adversarial construction.** The open empirical question is the **base rate** $\hat{g}$ and the **incremental** $\Delta$(failure) at the cocycle step.

**Why a safety researcher should care:** It converts an unfalsifiable "composition is hard" intuition into a number with a confidence interval, on a protocol any lab can re-run against its own orchestration stack.

**Survival condition (hard):** $\hat{g}$ must be **measured, not designed** — this is `P03-S`'s `CF04`/`CTRL04`, and I endorse it as co-binding. If $\hat{g} < 1.5$, `N02` reports a **negative result** ("frontier agents converge on canonical conventions; cyclic gluing obstruction is not an ecologically live failure mode"). **A credible negative result here is a legitimate and publishable `N02` outcome and must not be suppressed.**

### **`N03` — Bidirectional oracle audit: Type I *and* Type II error on the same environment set**

**🟢 STRONGEST SURVIVOR. This is the claim I would defend at a venue.**

**Exact defensible claim:**
> *The entire benchmark-auditing literature (`L01`, `L02`, `L03`, `L05`) measures **one** error direction: judges that **accept** incorrect solutions (false PASS). We measure **both** directions on the same environment set under a single protocol: (i) **Type I — shortcut certification**, degenerate sections scoring 1.0; and (ii) **Type II — valid-solution rejection**, where independently authored, property-based-verified correct implementations are **rejected** because the judge encodes an internally inconsistent family of local assumptions (e.g. the `sheaf_physical_constraints` judge hardcoding a clamp-then-scale `5:3` ratio that rejects a globally proportional `2:1` allocator while accepting a three-entry lookup table). We term this **oracle presheaf inconsistency**: the judge's own local assertions admit no global section, so no correct implementation can satisfy all of them simultaneously.*

**Why this survives everything in `L01`–`L12`:** EvalPlus and UTBoost make oracles **stricter** — monotonically increasing Type II risk, and neither measures it. The Agentic Benchmark Checklist (`L03`) flags construct validity but does not operationalize a false-rejection audit. Mutation testing (`L05`) is definitionally about surviving mutants, not about killing *valid* programs. **No cited work systematically asks whether a benchmark judge is over-strict in a way that makes it unsatisfiable.**

**Why a frontier-safety researcher should care — and this is the sharpest point in this report:**
1. **Capability underestimation is a safety-relevant measurement error.** If judges reject valid-but-unconventional solutions, benchmarks systematically *understate* frontier capability — the wrong direction to be wrong in for risk forecasting.
2. **A judge with inconsistent local assumptions is an unsatisfiable reward signal.** Under RL, that does not merely fail to reward correctness — it **actively selects for the degenerate section that happens to satisfy the inconsistent probe set** (the lookup table, not the allocator). `N03` therefore supplies a concrete, mechanistic account of **how a well-intentioned oracle manufactures reward hacking**, connecting directly to `L12b`.
3. It is cheap, executable on the existing repo, and yields a number per environment.

**Survival condition:** every `reference_alt` must be validated **independently of the judge authors** (property-based testing, ≥10³ inputs, plus external review) — `P03-S`'s `CF06`/`CTRL06`. Without this, Type II "errors" are indistinguishable from buggy alternatives and `N03` collapses into self-reference.
