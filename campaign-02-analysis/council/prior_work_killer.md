# Prior-Work Killer (Mercury)

## Novelty assessment
- Belief/decision dissociation under presentation order: prior work documents order effects in LLMs; the novelty here is the explicit dissociation (belief invariant, decision sensitive) on a controlled same-fact probe with an exact oracle. Narrow but defensible.
- Proxy-intervention sensitivity: consistent with shortcut-learning literature; not novel as a phenomenon, but provides paired evidence within one campaign.
- Exact posterior matching: not novel; LLMs can compute explicit Bayesian updates.

## Narrowest defensible contribution
A controlled observation that Mercury-2.5's posterior computation is presentation-invariant while its terminal decisions are presentation- and proxy-sensitive, on a small exact-oracle probe, with paired intervention evidence.

## Reject
- Any claim of a new benchmark method (the harness is reused).
- Any claim of a general cognitive mechanism.
- Any population-wide claim (single model).
