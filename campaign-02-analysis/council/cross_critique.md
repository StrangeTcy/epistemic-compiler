# Cross-critique (Mercury)

## Theorist responds to Skeptic
The Skeptic's small-sample concern is valid; the 0/36 belief-flip count is the more robust observation than 5/36 terminal flips. The dissociation claim should rest primarily on belief invariance plus terminal sensitivity, not on the exact terminal count.

## Experimentalist responds to Theorist
The two-stage architecture is an interpretation, not a measured mechanism. The probe measures endpoints, not internal stages. Label as interpretation (I-001).

## Skeptic responds to Prior-Work Killer
Agrees novelty is narrow; the value is the controlled dissociation, not a new method.

## Prior-Work Killer responds to Experimentalist
The paired intervention evidence strengthens the proxy finding but difficulty confounding remains; recommend difficulty-stratified analysis before publication.

## Synthesis
Strongest defensible finding: belief computation is presentation-invariant while decisions are presentation/proxy-sensitive (F-001, F-002). Unresolved: whether terminal flips are decision-layer or extraction artifacts; whether proxy effect survives difficulty matching. Preserve these as open questions.
