**Status markers**

- **Named** — identified in the dialogue but without a complete citation.
- **Linked** — an explicit URL appears in the dialogue.
- **Ambiguous association** — a URL is placed after a sentence naming multiple works, so the exact mapping is not certain from the dialogue alone.

- **Literary pointer** — fiction is being used as a thought experiment, not as empirical evidence.
